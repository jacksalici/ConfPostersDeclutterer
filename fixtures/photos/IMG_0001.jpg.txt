Deep Sets for Jet Flavour Tagging at the LHC
A. Rossi, B. Bianchi, C. Verdi, D. Neri
Department of Physics, University of Bologna and INFN
Abstract
We present a permutation-invariant deep learning architecture for jet flavour tagging.
The neural network is trained on simulated ATLAS collider events and improves the
trigger efficiency at high pileup. arXiv:2401.12345
Results
The cross section measurement agrees with the standard model prediction.
